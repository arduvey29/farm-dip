What we collect

When you order, we collect your name, phone number, email, delivery address and order details. Payments are processed by our payment partner; we never see or store your full card details.

How we use it

We use your data to deliver orders, send order updates, provide support and, if you opt in, send offers. We do not sell your personal data.

Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies to keep your cart working, and analytics to understand how our site is used. Your cart is stored in your browser.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Write to info.farmdip@gmail.com.
