Where we deliver

We deliver to most serviceable pincodes in India. If we cannot reach your pincode, we will call or WhatsApp you before dispatch.

Shipping charges

Shipping is free on orders above ₹999. Orders below that have a flat shipping charge of ₹79. Cash on delivery is free.

Dispatch and delivery times

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours on working days (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays).

Delivery usually takes 2 to 4 working days to metro cities and 4 to 7 working days elsewhere. You will receive tracking details by SMS, WhatsApp and email.

Packaging

Jars are packed in moulded pulp inserts inside double-wall cartons with airtight seals. If a parcel looks damaged, take a photo before opening it.
